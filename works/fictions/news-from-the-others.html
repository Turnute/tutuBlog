Résumé

Vendredi hunts because movement keeps the ghosts quiet. Nata imitates his hero in an effort to bury his own.
Seasons in the Forbidden Lands are unforgiving. And when storms drive the herds, panic makes poor choices as a crowned Apex stirs beneath the thunder.
A Monster Hunter story about what courage is for, the promises you keep when nobody’s watching, and the moment a legend leans down to admit, very softly, “I’m scared too.”
