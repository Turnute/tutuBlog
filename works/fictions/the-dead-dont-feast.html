Résumé

The Dead Don't Feast, And the living don’t rise. A dark fantasy story inspired by the world of Minecraft.

The Witch is dead. Her otherworldly, sculk-infused servant, Alice, buries her by midnight. By morning, a miracle is asked of her.
What happens when a tool that denies its own history is left behind to grieve the hand that gave it purpose?
