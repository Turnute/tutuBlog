Résumé

She wasn’t always like this.
You know, scared.


What happens after the story? When all is said and done, when the hero saves the day and goes home. What's next?
Juliana struggles to move on after the events surrounding the Crater and Blueberry Academy. Surrounded by visions of her failures and deeply traumatized by Terastallization, she faces the challenges of evoiding her trauma while pretending to be fine, the hero the whole region adores.
Everything goes sideways when a new threat surfaces. How will she fair, running from her past, from her friends, all the while facing this new opponent?
